## Standards & formats

### Incident reporting

**FEMA ICS-209** — Incident Status Summary
- https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/2020-07/fema_nims_ics_209-Form.pdf
- US-standard format for multi-agency incident reporting

**OCHA SITREP** — Situation Report
- https://www.unocha.org/situation-reports
- UN-standard format for humanitarian crisis reporting (international equivalent of ICS-209)

### Damage assessment

**HAZUS** — FEMA's natural-hazard loss-estimation methodology
- https://www.fema.gov/flood-maps/products-tools/hazus
- Categories: DS1 (Slight) → DS5 (Complete)

### Patient triage

**START** — Simple Triage And Rapid Treatment
- https://chemm.hhs.gov/startadult.htm
- Categories: Green (Minor), Yellow (Delayed), Red (Immediate), Black (Deceased)

### Emergency alerting

**Cell Broadcast (CBS)** — 3GPP standard for area-targeted emergency messaging
- https://www.3gpp.org/technologies/cbs
- Underlying transport for WEA (US), SACHET (India), IT-Alert (Italy), EU-Alert (EU)

**Common Alerting Protocol (CAP)** — OASIS standard for alert content
- https://www.oasis-open.org/standard/cap-v1-2/

**Wireless Emergency Alerts (WEA)** — US federal alert system
- https://www.fcc.gov/wireless-emergency-alerts-wea
